Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026. Applies to DrawWatch on Android and iPhone, all versions.

DrawWatch is a lottery ticket checker made by E4 Developers ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app keeps on your phone, what it sends, who receives it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • There's no account. Your tickets, pools and saved numbers stay on your phone.
  • If you allow it, a smaller copy of a ticket photo you scan is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to double-check the numbers. We don't keep it. You can turn this off.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase for alerts, crash reports and usage statistics. Those services collect device identifiers and similar data.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

What stays on your phone

  • Your tickets: numbers, game, add-ons, purchase date and how many draws each covers.
  • Group pools: the names you type in, what each person paid, and the lines.
  • Saved number sets, and the dates you type into Number ideas.
  • Your settings, and a copy of recent draw results so the app works offline.

We never receive this information. It's deleted when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app, except for copies in your phone's own backup (see Backups).

What leaves your phone, and why

Draw results

The app asks our server for the latest results and jackpots. These requests don't include your tickets or anything about you. Like any web server, ours sees your IP address while it answers. The server is hosted by Cloudflare. It gets results from LotteryData.io and from public official sources (powerball.com, megamillions.com and New York State's open data site, data.ny.gov). Nothing about you is sent to any of them.

Ticket scanning

  • The first time you scan, the app asks whether it may double-check scans online. If you say yes and your phone is online, a smaller copy of the ticket photo is sent to our server, which sends it to Anthropic's Claude to read the numbers and returns them. Your phone reads the photo at the same time.
  • Our server doesn't store the photo. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms: it doesn't use it to train its models, and it deletes it within 30 days, unless its safety systems flag the request, in which case it may keep it longer as described in Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • If you choose "Read on this phone only", or turn off "Double-check scans online" in Alerts & settings, or you're offline, the photo never leaves your phone. Reading on the phone uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your device.
  • To limit use, our server counts how many photos each install sends per day, using a random ID the app makes (or, if that's missing, your IP address). The counts are deleted after 2 days.
  • Versions before 2.0 never sent photos anywhere.

Notifications

Result alerts and draw-night alerts are sent by our server through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) to the games you choose. To do that, Firebase gives your phone a device token and records which alert topics you follow. Win alerts and jackpot alerts are worked out on your phone by comparing your tickets with the results. You can turn any of them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives a report: what went wrong, the device model, the operating system and app versions, and a random installation ID. It doesn't include your tickets.

Usage statistics

Firebase Analytics (Google) receives app opens, sessions and a few events, such as "a win was found" with the game's name, never your numbers. Versions before 2.0 also sent the smallest possible prize amount with that event; 2.0 doesn't. It uses an app-instance ID and, on Android, may use the advertising ID. We use these statistics only as totals, to see which parts of the app are used and to fix problems.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects the advertising ID and app set ID, approximate location from your IP address, device information, and how you interact with ads. It uses them to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent before personalized ads, using Google's consent tool.
  • Under some US state privacy laws, personalized ads can count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". Where Google's consent tool offers it, you can opt out in the app under Alerts & settings, Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the app shows non-personalized ads and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings at any time.

Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Backups

  • On Android, your tickets, pools, saved numbers and settings are included in your phone's backup to your Google account if you have backup turned on. Google stores and encrypts it, not us. On iPhone, your device or iCloud backup may include them in the same way.
  • "Save backup file" makes a file on your phone. Where it goes (Drive, email, Files) is up to you. We never see it.

How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Our server: no personal data is stored, apart from the daily scan counts, which are deleted after 2 days.
  • Anthropic: scan photos are deleted within 30 days, unless flagged as described above.
  • Google: according to Google's own retention settings and policies.

Your choices

  • Delete any ticket, pool or saved number set in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • Turn off online scan double-checks, alerts, or ad personalization in the app's settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us any question about your data, or ask us to delete anything we hold, by email (below). Because there's no account, we usually hold nothing that identifies you, but we'll help with any request, including requests under US state privacy laws or the GDPR.

Children

DrawWatch is meant for adults 18 and over and isn't directed to children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Security

Everything the app sends uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our data provider's key is kept on our server, never in the app. No method of storage or transfer is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect to a minimum.

Where data is processed

Our server and the services above run mainly in the United States. If you use the app from elsewhere, your data may be processed there.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also say so in the app.
