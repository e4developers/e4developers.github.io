Two apps. One small studio.

We make focused mobile tools: one for checking lottery tickets, one for home inspectors. Both store your data on your device, not on our servers, and neither uses what you put in to train AI.

DrawWatch

Lottery ticket checker · Android · Free

Scan a Powerball, Mega Millions, Lotto America or Millionaire for Life ticket. DrawWatch checks it against every draw it's in and shows what each line won.

  • Scan a ticket, or tap the numbers in
  • The prize for every line, add-ons included
  • Alerts when results are in and when a ticket wins
  • Free with ads, no account

DrawSight

Inspection reporting · iOS and Android · Subscription

Photo-first reporting for licensed home inspectors. AI sorts each photo into the right report section, drafts the finding text and suggests severity. The inspector reviews and ships.

  • Photo routing and finding drafts
  • Inspection data stays on the phone
  • State-aware PDF export with the inspector's seal
  • No AI training on inspection photos
