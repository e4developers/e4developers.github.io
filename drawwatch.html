How it works

  1. 1

    Scan your ticket

    Point the camera at it. Every line, add-on and draw date is read, and anything unclear is highlighted for you to check.

  2. 2

    DrawWatch follows the draws

    Results come in on their own for every draw your ticket covers, with an alert when they're in.

  3. 3

    See what you won

    Each line shows what it matched and what it pays, with Power Play, multipliers, All Star Bonus and Double Play worked in.

Is DrawWatch free?

Yes. DrawWatch is free and shows ads. There's no account and no sign-up.

Can I buy tickets in DrawWatch?

No. DrawWatch checks tickets you already bought from a lottery retailer. It doesn't sell tickets or take bets.

Are the prize amounts official?

DrawWatch shows each game's standard prizes, including Power Play, the Mega Millions multiplier, All Star Bonus and Double Play. Some prizes, and every prize in California, are pari-mutuel and can differ. Always confirm a win with your state lottery before you throw a ticket away.

Which states does it work in?

Anywhere these games are sold. Double Play and All Star Bonus are only offered in some states.

What happens to my ticket photos?

Your phone reads the photo. If you allow it, a smaller copy is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to double-check the numbers. We don't keep it, and you can switch to reading on your phone only at any time. See the privacy policy.

Is DrawWatch on iPhone?

Not yet. DrawWatch is on Android today.

Where can I get help with gambling?

If gambling stops being fun, free and confidential help is available. Call or text 1-800-GAMBLER.
