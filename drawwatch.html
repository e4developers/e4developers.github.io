How it works

  1. 1

    Scan your ticket

    Point the camera at it. Every line, add-on and draw date is read, and anything unclear is highlighted for you to check. You can also tap the numbers in.

  2. 2

    DrawWatch follows the draws

    Results come in on their own for every draw your ticket covers, and you get an alert when they're in.

  3. 3

    See what you won

    Each line shows the numbers it matched and its prize, with Power Play, multipliers, All Star Bonus and Double Play worked in.

Scan it once

One photo adds the whole ticket: every line, Power Play or Double Play, the multiplier, how many draws it's good for and the date.

  • Numbers that look off are ringed so you can check them
  • Edit anything before you save
  • No camera handy? Tap the balls instead

Watch your numbers on draw night

When results land, the balls drop one at a time and the numbers on your lines light up as they match.

  • Plays once per draw, and you can skip it
  • Ends on what you won, or a plain "no win this time"

Know exactly what each line won

Every draw has its prize table, with the rows you matched highlighted. Wins show the math, like $7 × 3 = $21.

  • Multi-line and multi-draw tickets stay together
  • Where to cash it and how long you have to claim

Run the office pool

Add who's in and what they put in. DrawWatch checks the pool's lines every draw and splits any prize to the cent.

  • See what each person paid and what each person gets
  • Share a summary with the group

Your numbers, by the numbers

Hot, cold and overdue numbers for each game, how often every number has come up, and what you've spent and won.

Draws are random. Stats describe the past, not what comes next.

Questions

Is DrawWatch free?

Yes. DrawWatch is free and shows ads. There's no account and no sign-up.

Can I buy tickets in DrawWatch?

No. DrawWatch checks tickets you already bought from a lottery retailer. It doesn't sell tickets or take bets.

Are the prize amounts official?

DrawWatch shows each game's standard prizes, including Power Play, the Mega Millions multiplier, All Star Bonus and Double Play. Some prizes, and every prize in California, are pari-mutuel and can differ. Always confirm a win with your state lottery before you throw a ticket away.

Which states does it work in?

Anywhere these games are sold. Double Play and All Star Bonus are only offered in some states.

What happens to my ticket photos?

Your phone reads the photo. If you allow it, a smaller copy is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to double-check the numbers. We don't keep it, and you can switch to reading on your phone only at any time. See the privacy policy.

Is DrawWatch on iPhone?

Not yet. DrawWatch is on Android today.

Where can I get help with gambling?

If gambling stops being fun, free and confidential help is available. Call or text 1-800-GAMBLER.
