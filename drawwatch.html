Did your ticket win?

Scan your lottery ticket. DrawWatch checks it against every draw it's good for and shows what each line won.

Get it on Google Play Free with ads. Android. No account.

What it does

  • Scan or tap in a ticketPoint the camera at the ticket. DrawWatch reads every line, the add-ons and the draw dates. Anything hard to read is highlighted so you can check it. You can also tap the numbers in yourself.
  • See what each line wonEvery ticket is checked against each draw it's good for, with the prize for each line, including Power Play, the Mega Millions multiplier, All Star Bonus and Double Play.
  • Draw nightWhen results come in, the balls land one at a time and the numbers on your lines light up.
  • AlertsA notification when results are in for the games you pick, another when a ticket wins, and one when a jackpot passes an amount you choose.
  • Office poolsWho's in, what each person paid, and what each person gets if a line wins.
  • Results and statsRecent draws for every game with their prize tables, how often each number comes up, and what you've spent and won.
  • BackupsSave your tickets to a file and restore them on a new phone. Android's own backup includes them too.

Your data

  • Your tickets stay on your phoneThere's no account to make, and your tickets, pools and saved numbers aren't uploaded to us.
  • ScanningIf you allow it, a smaller copy of the ticket photo is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to double-check the numbers. We don't keep the photo. You can switch to reading on your phone only at any time.
  • Ads and app healthThe app shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase for alerts, crash reports and usage statistics.

The details are in the privacy policy.

Please note

DrawWatch isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any lottery, and it doesn't sell tickets. Prize amounts shown are the standard prizes; some prizes, and every prize in California, are pari-mutuel and can differ. Always confirm a win with your official state lottery before you throw a ticket away.

Draws are random, and past results don't predict future ones. Please play responsibly. For help with problem gambling, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER.
