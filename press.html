Press facts

For reporters and writers. Updated October 26, 2026.

In one line

DrawWatch is a free app that scans a paper lottery ticket and checks it against every drawing it's in, so players don't throw away a winner or miss a claim deadline.

The facts

What it doesScan a ticket once. DrawWatch checks each line against every drawing the ticket covers, shows what each line won with the add-ons (Power Play, the Mega Millions multiplier, Double Play), and tracks the claim deadline after a win.
AlsoOffice and family lottery pools (who's in, who paid, each share), draw-night alerts, home-screen widgets, spending and winnings by month.
GamesPowerball, Mega Millions, Lotto America and Millionaire for Life, plus NY Take 5, NY Numbers, NY Win 4, NY Lotto, TX Cash Five, Lotto Texas, Texas Two Step and PA Cash 5.
PriceFree, supported by ads. No subscription, no account or sign-up.
PrivacyTickets stay on the phone. A scan can be double-checked by an AI service (the photo isn't kept by us); players can switch that off. See the privacy policy.
AvailabilityAndroid, on Google Play since 2026 (version 2.0 on October 26, 2026). iPhone version 2.0 is in Apple's review.
Made byE4 Developers, a small independent developer.
Not affiliatedDrawWatch isn't connected to or endorsed by any lottery and doesn't sell tickets. Winners should always confirm with their state lottery.
